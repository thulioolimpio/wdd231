GreenGrow Gardening – Site Plan

Site Name

GreenGrow Gardening

This name was selected to represent an eco-friendly gardening service that focuses on sustainability and home-based garden solutions. It's memorable and clearly tied to the theme of growth and nature.

Optional domain availability: greengrowgardens.com

Site Purpose

The website will provide resources, gardening tips, plant recommendations, and booking services for in-home garden consultations. It will also include a blog with seasonal gardening advice and a contact form for customers to reach out.

Scenarios

  • What are the best low-maintenance plants for indoor gardens?
  • How can I book a consultation for garden planning?

Color Schema

  • Forest Green (#228B22): Used for headers, navigation bar, and buttons.
  • Ivory (#FFFFF0): Background color for the page for readability and light contrast.

Typography

  • Heading Font: "Montserrat", sans-serif – Used for all headers and navigation.
  • Body Font: "Open Sans", sans-serif – Used for paragraphs and general text.
